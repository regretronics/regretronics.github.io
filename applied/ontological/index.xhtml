Project ontological

Introduction:

This project is inspired by my ramblings and investigations about the domain of meanings and their representation. I wanted to get rid of more concrete aspects that are usually embedded in communication, like phonemes, language tradition and even psychological or social concerns (like rhetoric and etiquette), for a highly conceptual and abstract form of content. It's a logical kind of content, that often interacts with consequences and decisions.

It's also inspired by a range of concepts including formal grammars, dependency syntax, knowledge representation, symbolic artificial intelligence, narrow data, ontological categories.

My strategy involves formatting content into a tree based mainly on a dualism between property and value, or question and answer. In this manner, the content gets broken down from a qualitative level to a quantitative one, ultimately getting to mathematical objects.

In this experiment, the objective is to build an autonomous fiction generator or an "imaginator", generating a virtual world (more or less following the real one) with its due parts. But in this case, the approach is very abstract (unlike generating pixels, for example, which is visual and rather shallow). This could have some creative and ludic applications, like increased replay value and flexibility from variable artificial situations in computer games.
